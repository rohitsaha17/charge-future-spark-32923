## For partners — site hosting

### CAPEX model
- Who funds hardware: **Partner**
- Who funds installation, software, ops, support: A Plus Charge
- Revenue share: Partner-favourable (majority to partner)
- Projected ROI: **15–20% over 5 years** for sites with qualifying daily traffic
- Typical capex per 60 kW DC station: ₹18–25 lakh including civil + transformer
- Typical capex per 7.4 kW AC bank (4 ports): ₹2.5–4 lakh
- Site approval lead time: 6–12 weeks (DISCOM + civil dependent)

### OPEX model
- Who funds hardware, installation, software, ops, support: **A Plus Charge**
- Who provides space, grid connection, security: Partner
- Revenue share: A Plus Charge-favourable (longer payback for A Plus Charge)
- Partner capex required: **None** — only land + utilities
- Lock-in: 5–7 years typical
- Suitable for: landlords without capex appetite; high-footfall properties

### Common to both models
- A Plus Charge handles DISCOM / municipal approvals, equipment, network, payments, 24×7 uptime monitoring, branded customer service.
- Stations co-branded with the partner (mall, hotel, fleet operator).
- Mobile-app discovery, real-time availability, in-app payments.
- Service-level commitment: monthly uptime ≥ 95%.
