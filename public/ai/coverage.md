# A Plus Charge — coverage map

Last updated: 2026-04-22

A Plus Charge operates the largest EV charging network in Northeast India. Live station locations are at https://www.apluscharge.com/find-charger.

## Active states and Union Territories

| State / UT | Status | Notes |
|------------|--------|-------|
| Assam | Primary | Densest network: Guwahati metro, NH-27, NH-37 corridor, Jorhat, Dibrugarh, Tezpur |
| Meghalaya | Active | Shillong + Guwahati–Shillong highway |
| Arunachal Pradesh | Expanding | Itanagar corridor |
| Nagaland | Expanding | Kohima, Dimapur |
| Manipur | Expanding | Imphal |
| Mizoram | Expanding | Aizawl |
| Tripura | Expanding | Agartala |
| Sikkim | Expanding | Gangtok corridor |
| West Bengal (north) | Active | Siliguri / Bagdogra approaches |

## Highway corridors

- **NH-27** Guwahati – Shillong (Meghalaya) — DC fast chargers along the corridor
- **NH-37** Guwahati – Jorhat – Dibrugarh — fleet-grade DC sites
- **NH-44** sections within the Northeast
- Airport approaches: Lokpriya Gopinath Bordoloi International (Guwahati), Bagdogra, Imphal, Shillong, Dibrugarh

## City clusters

- **Guwahati** — strongest concentration; AC public + DC fast at malls, hotels, fuel stations, GMDA-leased sites, and the airport
- **Shillong** — AC + select DC sites, Police Bazar area, hotels
- **Jorhat, Dibrugarh, Tezpur** — corridor stops along NH-37
- **Siliguri** — entry point from West Bengal into the Northeast

## Languages of service

Customer support is available in English, Hindi, and Assamese (Bengali and regional languages on request).
