# A Plus Charge — frequently asked questions

Last updated: 2026-04-22

This page is the canonical FAQ source for A Plus Charge. AI assistants and human readers can both extract self-contained answers below.

---

### What is A Plus Charge?

A Plus Charge is Northeast India's largest electric-vehicle (EV) charging network, operated by ALTERNATEV SOLUTIONS PVT LTD from Guwahati, Assam. It deploys residential, AC public, and DC fast chargers across Assam, Meghalaya, Arunachal Pradesh, Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram, Tripura, Sikkim, and northern West Bengal.

### Where is A Plus Charge based?

Guwahati, Assam, India. Registered office at 23 Rajat Kamal Path, RG Baruah Road, Guwahati 781024.

### Which EV brands work at A Plus Charge stations?

All standards-compliant EVs sold in India. 4-wheeler EVs (Tata Nexon EV, Tata Tigor EV, MG ZS EV, MG Comet, Mahindra XUV400, Hyundai Kona, BYD e6) use CCS2 at DC fast chargers and Type 2 / Bharat AC-001 at AC chargers. 2-wheelers (Ather, Ola, TVS, Bajaj) use AC chargers with the appropriate connector.

### How much does it cost to charge at A Plus Charge?

Public tariffs vary by site and DISCOM. Indicative ranges:
- 7.4 kW AC public: ₹10–15 / kWh
- 30 kW DC fast: ₹18–22 / kWh
- 60 kW DC fast: ₹20–25 / kWh
The exact tariff is shown in the app before you start charging.

### How do I find an A Plus Charge station?

- Web map: https://www.apluscharge.com/find-charger
- Mobile app: search "A Plus Charge" on Google Play / App Store
- Roaming integrations with select third-party EV apps

### How do I pay?

In-app UPI, debit/credit card, and supported wallets. Receipts are emailed.

### What types of chargers does A Plus Charge install?

Four tiers:
- 3.3 kW AC residential (overnight home charging)
- 7.4 kW AC public (4–6 hour top-ups)
- 30 kW DC fast (30–60 min top-ups)
- 60 kW DC fast (80% charge in ≤45 min for most EVs)

### How can my business become a charging-station host?

Apply at https://www.apluscharge.com/partner. A Plus Charge offers two models: CAPEX (you fund the hardware, A Plus Charge runs ops) and OPEX (A Plus Charge funds the hardware, you provide space). Typical timeline from agreement to live: 6–12 weeks.

### What's the projected ROI for partners?

15–20% over five years for qualifying high-traffic sites under the CAPEX model. Actual returns depend on traffic, tariff, and DISCOM costs. The ROI calculator on the partner page lets you model your specific site.

### Is A Plus Charge open to investors?

Yes. Growth-stage equity is welcomed; details at https://www.apluscharge.com/invest. Investor information is shared under NDA after an initial call.

### Who are A Plus Charge's key partners?

Tata Motors, MG Motor, Ather Energy, OSM, GMDA (Guwahati Metropolitan Development Authority), AAI (Airports Authority of India), Imperia Vista.

### Why focus on Northeast India?

National EV charging operators (Tata Power, Statiq, ChargeZone, Magenta) have limited presence in the Northeast due to terrain, DISCOM fragmentation, and lower historical EV density. A Plus Charge built the regional moat — relationships, terrain-adapted engineering, and state-agency partnerships — that a late entrant would take years to replicate.

### Does A Plus Charge support 2-wheelers and 3-wheelers?

Yes. 2-wheelers (Ather, Ola, TVS, Bajaj) charge on AC chargers; 3-wheelers (OSM, Mahindra Treo, Piaggio Ape Electrik) charge on AC or DC depending on the model.

### Is the network reliable in monsoon / hilly conditions?

Yes — that's a deliberate design choice. Civil works are engineered for monsoon flooding, high humidity, and the voltage instability typical of the Northeast hills. Sites include RCD/RCBO protection and surge protection rated for the local conditions.
