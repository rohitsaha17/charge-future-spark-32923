# A Plus Charge — service catalog

Last updated: 2026-04-22

A Plus Charge operates four tiers of electric-vehicle charging hardware across Northeast India. All hardware complies with Indian Bureau of Energy Efficiency standards and is supported under a single network operator (A Plus Charge / ALTERNATEV SOLUTIONS PVT LTD).

## 1. Residential — 3.3 kW AC

- Power: 3.3 kW single-phase AC
- Connector: Type 2 (universal) or Bharat AC-001
- Charge time: 6–10 hours (depends on EV battery)
- Use case: overnight charging at home
- Typical user: daily commuter, single-EV household
- Installation: A Plus Charge surveys the home, handles DISCOM load enhancement if required, installs the wall-box, and commissions on the network app.

## 2. AC Public — 7.4 kW

- Power: 7.4 kW single-phase AC
- Connector: Type 2 / Bharat AC-001
- Charge time: 4–6 hours for a full-cycle car, 2–3 hours for a 2-wheeler
- Use case: workplaces, malls, hotels, paid parking, residential apartment complexes
- Typical site: 2–8 ports per location
- Discovery: appears in the A Plus Charge app and supported EV roaming networks.

## 3. DC Fast — 30 kW

- Power: 30 kW DC
- Connector: CCS2 (primary), CHAdeMO (some sites)
- Charge time: 30–60 minutes for an 80% charge on a mid-range EV
- Use case: urban hubs, malls with high turnover, fleet bases
- Typical footprint: 1–4 ports per site

## 4. DC Fast — 60 kW

- Power: 60 kW DC (dual-gun on most installs)
- Connector: CCS2 (primary), CHAdeMO (regional support)
- Charge time: ≤45 minutes to 80% on most EVs sold in India
- Use case: highway corridors (NH-27 Guwahati–Shillong, NH-37 Guwahati–Jorhat), fleet depots, airports
- Site engineering: includes transformer upgrade, RCD/RCBO protection, surge protection rated for Northeast monsoon conditions

## Connector support summary

| Connector | Tiers |
|-----------|-------|
| Type 2 (Mennekes) | 3.3 kW, 7.4 kW |
| Bharat AC-001 | 3.3 kW, 7.4 kW |
| CCS2 | 30 kW, 60 kW |
| CHAdeMO | 30 kW (select sites) |

## Vehicles supported

- 4-wheeler EVs: Tata Nexon EV, Tata Tigor EV, MG ZS EV, MG Comet, Mahindra XUV400, Hyundai Kona, BYD e6, all CCS2-compatible vehicles
- 2-wheeler EVs: Ather 450/450X, Ola S1, TVS iQube, Bajaj Chetak (with adapter where required)
- 3-wheeler EVs: OSM Stream, Mahindra Treo, Piaggio Ape Electrik

## Software and network features

- Mobile app for station discovery (iOS + Android)
- Real-time availability and reservation
- In-app UPI / card payment
- Charge-history and receipts
- Roaming with select third-party networks
- 24×7 customer support in English, Hindi, and Assamese
