## What we operate

A regional EV charging network with four hardware tiers (3.3 kW, 7.4 kW, 30 kW, 60 kW), a unified software stack (discovery, payments, customer support), and exclusive partnerships with state agencies. We were the first-mover in public DC fast charging in Northeast India.

## Market thesis

India's EV market is projected to grow at ~49% CAGR through 2030 (NITI Aayog, FICCI estimates). Northeast India is structurally under-served by national operators because of:

- Hilly terrain and monsoon engineering complexity
- DISCOM fragmentation across 8 states
- Lower historical EV density (changing fast)
- Cultural / linguistic distance from Tier-1 cities

These are barriers to entry for late movers. A Plus Charge has spent capital and time building the regional moat — relationships, terrain expertise, regional fleet pipeline — that a national operator would now take 2–3 years to replicate.
